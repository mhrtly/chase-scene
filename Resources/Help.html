Chase Scene 🎷

Chase music whenever an AI drives your Mac.

Get started

Choose Settings → Connect Claude Code… or Connect Codex…. For other tools, choose Copy MCP server config and paste it into your AI's settings. Recognized desktop-control signals start the original chase theme; an idle AI conversation does not.

  • Claude Code: start a new session after connecting.
  • Codex: review and approve the added hooks with /hooks, then start a new session.

The menu's Chase music toggle and volume slider control sound. Rolling credits independently enables fictional task-related jokes over your desktop. Open at login is under Settings.

Rolling credits

Settings → Credits options offers a full-screen or corner layout and a silent 16-second preview. Large white letters with a dark outline and shadow scroll continuously from bottom to top. Each credit includes the current activity. Fresh updates enter without restarting the roll or repeating a fixed six-name reel. The transparent overlay does not take focus or intercept clicks. Choose an OTF or TTF font file to customize the lettering.

The included Chewy font is an approximation, not a verified match to the Benny Hill credits. The softened, lower-resolution rendering adds subtle scanlines and grain to every font.

The music

The original mouse_control_theme.mp3 is bundled as the default. Restore it with Use Original chase theme. Use Hot Potato Hustle selects Spudnik's alternative composition. Choose your own song… accepts MP3, M4A, WAV and AIFF.

Control ends or the signal is lost

Music and credits stop when the last live session ends, is interrupted or expires. An orange ? preserves a lost-signal warning until a fresh control start or you clear it. Restarting the app and late hook renewals do not resume stale playback. A signal is an indicator, not an input lock or a guarantee that your AI has stopped.

Optional mouse detection

Detect software mouse input (experimental) is off by default on new installs. It can notice posted mouse events but cannot reliably identify AI control. Ordinary utilities can trigger it; choose Not an AI? Ignore “…” or switch it off. Detected activity ends about 20 seconds after input stops. Upgrades keep existing preferences.

MCP and funny task credits

The MCP tools are begin_control, set_credits, keepalive, end_control and control_status. Pass a short, non-sensitive task to get related built-in puns, or supply custom rows such as {"role":"Legal advice","name":"Dewey, Cheatham and Howe"}. The controlling AI should supply fresh credits whenever its workflow changes; it can also put a one-line // chase-credits: JSON comment at the start of a JS computer-use action. Local task-aware puns fill gaps. These are templates; the app makes no additional AI inference calls. Preparing credits never starts control or music.

Begin before desktop actions, renew during long pauses, and end after pending actions finish. Without renewals, the default lease expires after three minutes. Use a new begin after a lost signal.

Privacy

Everything stays on this Mac: no telemetry, screen recording or external AI calls. Hooks inspect event names and bounded selected tool arguments to derive broad topics; they do not store raw arguments or read conversation transcripts. Preferences, session identifiers and short credit labels are stored locally. Mouse detection reads the posting process ID. The app never blocks or injects input and cannot stop an AI.

Connect/disconnect saves a backup and edits only Chase Scene's own entries. Settings shows the last received signal; that is not proof that every control tool is covered.
